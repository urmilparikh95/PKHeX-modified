# Generation 4 held items

What **Add Held Items** gives: one of each item below, in the Items pouch. Berries and mail are left out. The items are the held-item block of the Gen 4 item list, plus Oval Stone and the three orbs.

## Held evolution items

| Item | Evolves | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Metal Coat | Onix → Steelix; Scyther → Scizor | Trade |
| Dragon Scale | Seadra → Kingdra | Trade |
| King’s Rock | Poliwhirl → Politoed; Slowpoke → Slowking | Trade |
| Upgrade | Porygon → Porygon2 | Trade |
| Deep Sea Tooth | Clamperl → Huntail | Trade |
| Deep Sea Scale | Clamperl → Gorebyss | Trade |
| Electirizer | Electabuzz → Electivire | Trade |
| Magmarizer | Magmar → Magmortar | Trade |
| Protector | Rhydon → Rhyperior | Trade |
| Reaper Cloth | Dusclops → Dusknoir | Trade |
| Dubious Disc | Porygon2 → Porygon-Z | Trade |
| Razor Claw | Sneasel → Weavile | Level up at night |
| Razor Fang | Gligar → Gliscor | Level up at night |
| Oval Stone | Happiny → Chansey | Level up during the day |

Prism Scale (Feebas → Milotic) and Sachet/Whipped Dream are later-generation items, so they aren't included.
